## 6. Medical and safety caution

These documents are useful for understanding product claims and hardware/software concepts. They do not prove broad medical effectiveness.

Do not use Metatron, MORA, or Zapper equipment for:

```text
Tuberculosis treatment
Cancer treatment
Infection treatment
Parasite treatment
Height increase
Diagnosis instead of medical tests
Replacing physician care
```

For engineering work, use these documents as background only. Build only bench-test prototypes unless a device is properly designed, tested, and certified for human use.
